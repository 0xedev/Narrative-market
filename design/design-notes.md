# Narrative Markets visual direction

The frontend was designed from an Imagen-generated concept before implementation.

## Visual system

- Background: charcoal black with forest-green panels.
- Primary action: electric lime `#a3ff12`.
- Auction price and rank accents: warm gold `#f4b51b`.
- Typography: large condensed-feeling uppercase question headlines, compact utility labels, strong numeric hierarchy.
- Interaction language: sports scoreboard energy, visible countdowns, live activity, a prominent Throne state, and short competitive copy.
- Product boundary: it should feel like a social arena, not a financial trading terminal.
